KOReader Progress Sync (KOSync)

Sync Engine

Sync your reading positions, percentage, and timestamps seamlessly between your Kindle / e-Reader and Reading HQ.

1
Open any book in KOReader, tap the top of the screen to reveal the menu bar.
2
Tap the Tools (wrench icon) tab → select Progress sync.
3
Tap Custom sync server and enter server address:
Local LAN http://192.168.31.15:7200
Production WAN https://kosync.wavypremium.xyz
Standby VPS https://kosync-198-46-146-17.sslip.io
4
Tap Login and enter Username: moe (password as configured).
5
Enable "Automatically keep documents in sync" for continuous background synchronization.
6
Tap Pull progress to this device now to verify synchronization with Reading HQ.

KOReader OPDS Catalog (Direct Download)

Calibre Feed

Browse, search, and download books directly from your Calibre library into KOReader over Wi-Fi without cables.

1
In KOReader, tap top menu → Search icon (magnifying glass) → OPDS catalog.
2
Tap Add new catalog.
3
Set Catalog Name to: Mac Mini Library.
4
Enter Catalog URL:
Local LAN http://192.168.31.15:8088/opds
Standby VPS https://calibre-198-46-146-17.sslip.io/opds
5
Tap Connect (no username or password needed on your home LAN).

Kindle Wi-Fi Proxy Gateway (GFW Bypass)

sing-box

If your Kindle displays "Check your network connection" or fails to connect to overseas/standby VPS endpoints due to DPI/GFW firewalls, route Kindle Wi-Fi traffic through the local Mac Mini proxy:

1
On your Kindle: Go to Settings → Wi-Fi Networks.
2
Select your connected Wi-Fi network → tap Advanced or edit network configuration.
3
Configure HTTP Proxy:
Proxy Host 192.168.31.15
Proxy Port 7890
